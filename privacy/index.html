Cube Coach Privacy Policy

Retro Drop Games · Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: Cube Coach doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking, and it never sends anything over the internet. Everything happens on your device.

The camera

Cube Coach uses your camera to read the colors of your cube's stickers while you scan it. On a Mac, it also uses the camera while you solve, to check each turn you make.

The camera picture is looked at live, a few times a second, to measure sticker colors. It is never recorded, saved as a photo or video, or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in your device's Settings; you can still enter your cube's colors by hand.

What's saved on your device

To remember your preferences, Cube Coach saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your animation speed;
  • on a Mac, whether instructions are read aloud and which voice is used;
  • whether you've already seen the introduction;
  • the colors it has learned for your cube: a short list of numbers describing how each sticker color looks through your camera (not pictures).

This information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase the learned colors with Forget Learned Colors in Cube Coach's settings, and deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Reading instructions aloud

On a Mac, Cube Coach can read each turn aloud using the voices built into macOS. Speech is generated on your Mac; nothing is recorded, and Cube Coach doesn't use your microphone.

Testing with TestFlight

If you're testing a beta version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share some information with us, such as crash reports, basic device details, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple handles this as described in its privacy policy and TestFlight terms. We use it only to fix problems and improve Cube Coach.

Children

Because Cube Coach collects no personal information from anyone, it's suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If Cube Coach ever starts handling information differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
