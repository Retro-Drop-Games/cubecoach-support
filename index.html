Scanning isn't reading my colors right

Use Teach my colors on the home screen so Cube Coach learns how your cube looks in your light. Bright, even light helps, and so does filling the on-screen grid with one side of the cube. You can always fix a square by tapping it on the check screen.

Which cubes work?

Standard 3×3 cubes with the usual colors: white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, and red opposite orange.
